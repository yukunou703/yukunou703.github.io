Cell-free chromatin · Computational genomics

Hi, I’m Yukun.

I study how cell-free DNA and chromatin reveal gene regulation in cancer. In the Baca Lab and the Freedman Lab at Dana-Farber Cancer Institute, I build computational methods for circulating chromatin and test them with wet-lab experiments.

Next, I want to understand why patients respond differently to cancer therapy by bringing together several layers of patient data: tumour genomics and epigenomics, spatial and single-cell profiles of the tumour microenvironment, and clinical outcomes, working closely with wet-lab, pathology and clinical collaborators. My training so far spans epigenomics, machine learning and cancer biology, in academic research and in oncology drug discovery.
